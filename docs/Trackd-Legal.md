## 1. Terms of Service

### 1.1 Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on TRACKD or by using any part of the TRACKD platform, users agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. Use of the platform constitutes full and unconditional acceptance of these Terms. Users who do not agree must not create an account or use the platform.

These Terms may be updated from time to time. Users will be notified of material changes via the email address associated with their account and will be required to re-accept updated Terms before continuing to use the platform.

### 1.2 Service Description

TRACKD provides a web-based job application tracking, AI-powered analysis, and application material generation platform. The service is delivered on a freemium basis comprising a Free tier and a Pro subscription tier, as described on the pricing page.

TRACKD does not act as a recruiter, employment agency, or job board. The platform facilitates personal job hunt organisation and does not mediate between users and employers.

### 1.3 Eligibility

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum age | Users must be at least 16 years of age |
| Jurisdictional restrictions | The platform is not intended for use in jurisdictions where access would violate local law |
| Account type | Accounts are for individual personal use; corporate or multi-seat accounts are not offered in Phase 1 |

### 1.4 Account Obligations

Users agree to:

- Provide accurate, current, and complete information at registration and during onboarding
- Maintain the security of their account credentials and not share login access
- Notify TRACKD immediately of any suspected unauthorised access to their account
- Use the platform only for lawful personal job search and career management activities
- Not attempt to reverse-engineer, scrape, or automate interactions with the TRACKD platform beyond its intended use

Users must not use TRACKD to:

- Submit fraudulent or misleading information to employers
- Circumvent employer application screening processes in ways that misrepresent the user's identity or qualifications
- Generate application materials on behalf of another person without their knowledge and consent
- Conduct any activity that would impose an unreasonable load on TRACKD infrastructure

- Does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, suitability, or fitness for purpose of any AI-generated output
- Does not guarantee that AI-generated cover letters or resumes will improve employment outcomes
- Is not liable for any employment decision made by an employer on the basis of materials generated through the platform

Match scores and skills analyses are algorithmic estimates based on pattern matching. They do not constitute professional career advice.

### 2.6 Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Account and profile data | Duration of account + 30-day buffer post-deletion |
| Job application data | Duration of account + 30-day buffer post-deletion |
| Resume files (Cloud Storage) | Until re-uploaded, account deletion, or explicit file deletion — whichever comes first |
| AI-generated documents | 7 days from generation; regenerated on demand |
| Anonymised usage analytics | Up to 24 months |
| Billing records | 7 years (Nigerian tax law obligation) |
| Security and audit logs | 12 months |

### 2.7 Data Security

TRACKD applies the following technical and organisational security measures:

- All data encrypted at rest (Firestore native encryption; Cloud Storage server-side encryption)
- All data in transit encrypted via TLS 1.2 minimum
- Firebase Auth tokens expire and are refreshed according to Firebase's standard rotation policy
- Access to production databases and infrastructure is restricted to authorised VybzTech engineering personnel only
- All production access is logged and audited
- Firestore security rules enforce per-user data isolation at the database rule level

### 2.8 International Data Transfers

TRACKD's infrastructure (GCP, Firebase, Stripe) is primarily hosted in the United States. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or other regions with data transfer restrictions should be aware that their data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. TRACKD relies on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal transfer mechanism for data transferred from the EEA.

## 3. Cookie Policy

### 3.1 What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored on a user's device by their browser when they visit a website. TRACKD uses cookies and similar technologies to operate the platform and improve the user experience.

### 3.2 Cookies Used

| Category | Cookie / Technology | Purpose | Duration | Can Be Rejected? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | Firebase session token | Maintains the authenticated session | Session / 1 hour (auto-refreshed) | No — service cannot function without this |
| Essential | CSRF token | Protects against cross-site request forgery | Session | No |
| Functional | viewMode preference | Remembers the user's last dashboard view selection | 1 year | No (localStorage — not a cookie; minimal privacy impact) |
| Functional | sidebarCollapsed state | Remembers sidebar expansion preference | 1 year | No (localStorage) |
| Analytics | Anonymous session identifier | Tracks page views and feature usage; no PII attached | 90 days | Yes — opt out in notification preferences |
| Analytics | Event tracking | Records which features are used; aggregated only | 90 days | Yes — opt out in notification preferences |
| Marketing | Third-party ad pixels | Attribution for paid marketing campaigns | Varies | Yes — not used in Phase 1; will be disclosed if introduced |

### 3.3 Managing Cookie Preferences

Users can manage analytics cookie preferences in the Notification Preferences section of the Profile page. Browsers can also be configured to block all third-party cookies. Blocking essential cookies will prevent the platform from functioning.

## 5. AI-Generated Content Policy

### 5.1 How AI Is Used

TRACKD uses Google Gemini to:

- Extract structured data from job descriptions and resume documents
- Generate resume bullet point suggestions tailored to a specific job description
- Generate cover letter text based on the user's profile and a job description
- Produce match scores comparing a user's profile against a job's requirements

### 5.2 User Responsibilities

- Users are solely responsible for reviewing all AI-generated content before submitting it in a job application
- Users must ensure that AI-generated cover letters and resume edits accurately represent their actual skills, qualifications, and experience
- Submitting AI-generated content that misrepresents the user's qualifications to an employer is the user's responsibility and may constitute fraud in certain jurisdictions
- TRACKD does not verify the accuracy of AI-generated content against the user's actual background

### 5.3 AI Processing Consent

During the onboarding flow, users are presented with a clear disclosure that resume text and job description content will be processed by Google Gemini (a third-party AI model). Users must actively confirm this consent before the platform will process any of their data through the AI layer. This consent is logged with a timestamp and stored against the user's record.

Users who withdraw consent must delete their account, as AI processing is core to the platform's operation.

### 5.4 AI Output Limitations

- Match scores are algorithmic estimates and do not constitute professional career advice
- Skills gap analyses are based on keyword matching and may not reflect genuine competency requirements
- ATS keyword risk assessments are indicative and cannot guarantee or predict the behaviour of any specific employer's applicant tracking system
- Cover letters and resume suggestions are generated from a general-purpose AI model and may not reflect industry-specific norms or conventions in all fields
